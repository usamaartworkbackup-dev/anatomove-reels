Ever stood up too fast and seen stars? ✨😵

The second you stand, gravity pulls roughly half a litre of blood down into your legs and belly. Your blood pressure dips for a moment, so less blood reaches your brain: hello dizziness and dark vision.

Your body fights back fast:
❤️ your heart speeds up
🩸 your blood vessels tighten
🦵 your calf muscles squeeze blood back up
In most people it settles within about 15 seconds.

✅ To avoid it:
• Get up in stages: lying, then sitting, then standing
• Pump your ankles a few times before you stand
• Take extra care first thing in the morning
• Drink enough water

⚠️ If it keeps happening, or you faint, see a doctor.
